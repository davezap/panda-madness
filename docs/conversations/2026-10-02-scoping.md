### Assistant

Keep the panda itself totally platform agnostic. Simple sprite sheet plus a tiny state machine. Then treat each OS as a thin windowing adapter. Wayland is the strict case you design around, no global cursor snooping. For a desktop buddy, don't aim for full 30 FPS hand-drawn animation. That's just overkill. Classic 2D works on twos or threes. ### Assistant

#### Superman Panda Crash Sequence

| Step | Beat | Visual | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **FLY** | 🐼➜ | **Superman pose.** Smooth screen motion at 60 FPS. Only 2–4 art frames needed. |
| 2 | **BONK** | 🐼💥│ | **Impact squash.** One exaggerated flattened frame against the edge, held about 100–150 ms. |
| 3 | **STUNNED** | 🐼│ | **Tiny pause.** Hold him there for roughly 250–400 ms. The pause sells the joke. |
| 4 | **SLIDE** | ↘🐼│ | **Slow wall slide.** Same pose can move downward smoothly for 0.8–1.5 s with just a little wobble. |
| 5 | **FLOP** | 💫🐼 | **Heap on the floor.** Short drop, splat pose, then stay crumpled for a second before recovering. |

**Art budget:** about **6–10 unique panda drawings**. The app supplies the smooth flying, falling and sliding motion; the drawings supply the character. Timing is everything — hold the bonk for just a hair too long.

#### Curious Panda Interaction

| Step | Beat | Visual | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NOTICE** | 🐼 👀 🪟 | Panda spots a nearby window edge or other climbable target. |
| 2 | **APPROACH** | 🐼➜🪟 | Walks over and looks up at it before committing. |

| 5 | **ROLL** | ↶ 🐼 ↶ | Tumbles backward across the desktop with 3–4 rolling poses. |
| 6 | **RECOVER** | 💫🐼 | Ends in a heap, blinks, then gets back up as if nothing happened. |

| Design area | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Behaviour layer** | This introduces a simple desktop “scene model”: find nearby objects, classify them as ledges/obstacles, choose one, and run the appropriate gag. |
| **Portability rule** | Keep scene awareness behind an OS adapter. Start with ordinary window rectangles; desktop icons can be a later capability because they’re much more platform-specific, especially under Wayland. |

**Good V1 boundary:** panda can interact with screen edges and visible application windows. **Later:** desktop icons, taskbars/docks, window contents, and richer object recognition.

#### Panda Startled by a Dialog

| Step | Beat | Visual | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CALM** | 🐼 | Panda is idling, eating, sleeping, or wandering. |
| 2 | **POPUP** | ⚠️🪟 | A new dialog, alert, or sudden foreground window appears. |
| 3 | **STARTLE** | 😳🐼⬆️ | Instant jump, ears up, maybe a one-frame squash-and-stretch reaction. |
| 4 | **RETREAT** | 🐼💨 | Scuttles a short distance away or ducks behind the nearest edge. |
| 5 | **PEEK** | 👀🐼 | Pauses, cautiously looks back, then resumes normal behaviour. |

**Implementation:** the OS adapter reports events such as “new top-level window appeared” or “foreground window changed.” The panda engine decides whether that event is surprising enough to trigger the startle sequence. On Windows, start by reacting to new top-level windows/dialogs becoming visible or taking foreground focus; later distinguish a boring app switch from an error dialog or notification.

#### Panda Drag & Throw

| Step | Beat | Visual | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| 4 | **FLY** | 🐼↗️↻ | Floaty ballistic arc, gentle rotation, ears and paws trailing behind. |
| 5 | **BOUNCE** | 🐼↘️〰️ | Soft squash, tiny bounce or roll, never a violent impact. |
| 6 | **RECOVER** | 🐼😒 | Sits up, looks mildly offended, shakes itself off, then resumes life. |

| Design area | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Physics** | Simple 2D position, velocity, gravity and rotation. Clamp launch speed and damp each bounce so the panda always settles quickly. |
| **Animation** | Only a few extra drawings are needed — floppy-held pose, airborne pose, tumble pose, squash/landing pose and annoyed recovery. |

It reads playful, not mean, if the panda always pops back up unfazed.
